# Feature Ideas

This document is an idea bank, not a release commitment. Candidates should be validated against usability, privacy, maintenance cost, and battery impact before entering the product backlog.

## High-Value Candidates

- **Personal-record timeline:** Show rep, volume, duration, and estimated one-rep-max records with clear dates and exercise filters.
- **Plateau detection:** Identify stalled progression only after enough comparable sessions and explain the evidence behind the suggestion.
- **Goal forecasting:** Estimate when a fitted trend may cross a target, including uncertainty and a warning when the data is insufficient.
- **Per-muscle recovery view:** Combine recent volume, intensity, and elapsed time into an explainable readiness overview rather than a medical claim.
- **Workout templates and supersets:** Support reusable sequences, paired exercises, and rest timers without slowing down ordinary set entry.
- **Training annotations:** Add injury, illness, travel, deload, and equipment-change markers to make unusual plot periods understandable.

## Analytics

- Estimated one-rep max and relative-strength trends
- Weekly hard-set and volume trends by body part
- Session density, average rest time, and time-per-set trends
- Consistency calendar and missed-training patterns
- Exercise balance and antagonist-pair comparisons
- Acute versus longer-term workload with conservative explanations
- Correlations between body measurements and selected strength metrics
- Confidence bands and data-quality indicators for fitted trends

## Body Measurements

- Measurement-source filtering for manual and Health Connect data
- Configurable rolling-average windows and comparison overlays
- Waist, chest, hip, limb, and custom measurement types
- Target ranges with optional deadlines and projected crossing dates
- Reminders based on consistency rather than fixed daily notifications
- Clear distinction between measured lean body mass and calculated muscle estimates

## Usability and Accessibility

- Exercise search, tags, favorites, and equipment filters
- Home-screen quick-log widget and app shortcuts
- Optional wearable companion for rest timers and set confirmation
- Larger-text and high-contrast chart modes
- Exportable progress summaries that exclude private notes by default
- Contextual undo for destructive edits

## Suggested Evaluation Order

1. Personal-record timeline and training annotations
2. Plateau detection and goal forecasting
3. Workout templates and supersets
4. Per-muscle recovery and workload analytics
5. Widgets or wearable support after measuring demand and battery cost
